# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and generous with explanations. Assume that learners have different backgrounds, constraints and levels of English fluency.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, sharing private information and repeated personal attacks are not.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant. Credit original authors, disclose self-promotion and avoid spam. Give feedback about the work and its evidence rather than a person’s worth or ability.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary. If a public issue is safe and appropriate, flag a conduct concern there without exposing private details; otherwise use GitHub’s reporting tools. Do not publish sensitive details to report an incident.
